# Product Context: Udaman

## Why This Project Exists

### Problem Statement
Families and groups worldwide lack a comprehensive digital platform to:
- Create and manage multi-event competitions with sophisticated scoring
- Facilitate friendly rivalry and camaraderie through structured competition
- Track historical performance and maintain competitive traditions
- Scale competition management beyond manual organization
- Monetize competition management tools and services

### Target Audience
1. **Competition Creators**: Individuals or groups wanting to organize competitions
2. **Competition Participants**: People invited to participate in events
3. **Family Groups**: Multi-generational families seeking structured competition
4. **Social Groups**: Friends, clubs, and organizations wanting competitive activities
5. **Event Organizers**: People managing complex multi-event competitions

### Value Proposition
Udaman democratizes the tradition of friendly competition by providing a comprehensive digital platform that enables anyone to create, manage, and participate in sophisticated multi-event competitions with professional scoring, social features, and historical tracking.

## How It Should Work

### User Experience Goals

#### For Competition Creators
- **Setup**: Intuitive competition creation with event configuration
- **Management**: Easy participant invitation and permission management
- **Scoring**: Simple score entry with automatic point calculation
- **Analytics**: Insights into competition performance and engagement
- **Monetization**: Clear value proposition for premium features

#### For Competition Participants
- **Invitation**: Seamless invitation acceptance and account creation
- **Information**: Clear understanding of events, rules, and scoring
- **Participation**: Easy event participation and result submission
- **Social**: Engaging banter and rivalry features
- **History**: Access to personal and competition historical data

#### For Family/Social Groups
- **Tradition**: Tools to maintain and grow competitive traditions
- **Flexibility**: Support for diverse event types and scoring systems
- **Scalability**: Ability to grow competitions as groups expand
- **Accessibility**: Easy onboarding for new participants

### Core User Journeys

#### Competition Creator Journey
1. **Registration**: Sign up via OIDC social login or email
2. **Competition Setup**: Create new competition with basic details
3. **Event Configuration**: Add events with rules, requirements, and scoring
4. **Participant Invitation**: Send invitations to contenders via email
5. **Score Management**: Enter results and manage competition progress
6. **Analytics**: Review competition performance and participant engagement

#### Competition Participant Journey
1. **Invitation**: Receive email invitation to join competition
2. **Account Creation**: Accept invitation and create account
3. **Competition Overview**: Review events, rules, and scoring system
4. **Event Participation**: Participate in events and submit results
5. **Social Interaction**: Engage in banter and rivalry with other participants
6. **Results Tracking**: View personal performance and overall standings

#### Premium User Journey
1. **Feature Discovery**: Explore premium features and benefits
2. **Subscription**: Choose appropriate premium tier
3. **Payment**: Complete secure payment process
4. **Feature Access**: Unlock advanced functionality
5. **Value Realization**: Experience enhanced competition management

### Key Interactions
- **Information Architecture**: Clear, logical organization of content
- **Navigation**: Intuitive menu structure and search functionality
- **Responsive Design**: Seamless experience across all devices
- **Performance**: Fast loading times and smooth interactions
- **Accessibility**: Usable by people with disabilities

## Success Metrics
- **User Acquisition**: New user registrations and competition creations
- **Engagement**: Competition participation rates and feature usage
- **Retention**: Return visits and continued competition management
- **Monetization**: Premium subscription conversion rates
- **Social**: Participant interaction and banter engagement
- **Scalability**: Number of concurrent competitions and participants

## Competitive Landscape
- **Differentiation**: Multi-event competition management with sophisticated scoring
- **Quality**: Professional platform with social features and historical tracking
- **Flexibility**: Support for diverse event types and scoring systems
- **Community**: Focus on friendly rivalry and camaraderie building
- **Monetization**: Freemium model with clear value proposition
